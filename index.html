About — the short version

I'm a high school student at Mearns Castle. I spend most of my free time writing code — mostly Python and TypeScript — and figuring out how AI agents can be made genuinely useful rather than just impressive on a demo.

Outside the screen, I play badminton at a competitive level, cricket with friends, and have a stubborn weakness for a long board-game evening.

Now

Currently shipping pluggable AI-agent skills as a part-time contributor to Globalion's marketplace, running a YouTube channel called The Agent Lab, and preparing for university applications in maths & computer science.
03

Work — & where to find me

YouTube GitHub LinkedIn

Outside school, I contribute pluggable "skills" to Globalion Technology Solutions — a few evenings a week, on my own terms, not as an employee. Each skill is a small, self-contained MCP service that plugs into their agent platform; the fun is making them useful enough that people actually reach for them.

Several of the skills I've contributed are live in the platform today — covering messaging automation, scheduled tasks, document retrieval, and anonymous web fetching. Alongside the skills, I've helped shape the deployment stack behind them: containerised services on Hetzner, fronted by Cloudflare tunnels, with OAuth and Stripe-metered billing wired end-to-end. Real production work, done in the evenings.
